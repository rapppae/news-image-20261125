## The five stories

1. **Ukraine strikes Russian ships at sea** — President Zelenskiy confirms a drone strike on a Russian cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland; Kyiv also says it sank a Russian warship off Libya. Moscow calls the attacks terrorism.
2. **COP31 ends with no deal** — The UN climate summit in Antalya, Turkey collapses as oil and gas producers block fossil-fuel phase-out language.
3. **Huang back at Nvidia** — Nvidia's board reinstates Jensen Huang as chief executive after President Trump intervened; AI shares slide in Asia.
4. **Houthis hit Riyadh tower** — A strike on Riyadh's tallest tower wounds 11 people; Houthi forces advance to within 60 km of Aden in Yemen.
5. **Australia–Solomon Islands pact** — A security deal names Australia the Solomon Islands' primary partner and rules out foreign military bases.

Also noted on the poster: the reported Gaza death toll of 74,746; NATO warning over reports of a Russian nuclear-armed satellite; South Korea's education minister resigning over an exam-cheating scandal.

Sources: Reuters, AP and other wire reports of 25 November 2026.
